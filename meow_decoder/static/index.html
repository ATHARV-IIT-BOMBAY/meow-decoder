    How it works

    Cats don't speak in sentences, so this doesn't translate. It hears the meow and guesses which situation it sounds like, using a model trained on 440 meows from 21 cats recorded while waiting for food, being brushed, or alone somewhere unfamiliar.

    On cats it has never heard, the right situation is its top guess about 2 times in 3 and in its top two about 9 times in 10. Teaching it a few meows from your own cat pushed the top guess to about 8 in 10 in our tests. Its percentages are honest: a 90% answer is right about 9 times in 10.

    It also recognises purring, hissing and yowling directly, using a general sound model trained on thousands of clips. You can teach it more situations, like greeting you or wanting to go out, but those only work for cats you have taught. It can't tell pain from other sounds: in our tests, models that seemed to were recognising the recording, not the cat. If a cat seems hurt or unwell, ask a vet, not an app.
